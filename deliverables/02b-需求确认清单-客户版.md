## Important Notes for Your Attention

Please review the following before we proceed to a formal quotation. These points affect cost, timeline, and risk, and we prefer to be transparent about them from the start.

**Payment provider approval is not guaranteed.** Luxury and fashion goods are a closely monitored category. Payment providers carry out underwriting checks and will typically request brand authorisation documents and proof of product sourcing. We will assist with the application, but the final decision rests with the provider. We therefore recommend preparing these documents early.

**New high-value payment accounts may be subject to temporary fund holds.** This is standard risk practice for newly opened accounts processing high-value orders. We recommend planning working capital accordingly for the first weeks of trading.

**Tax and legal compliance remains your responsibility.** We implement the technical requirements (tax display, duty collection, consent mechanisms), but VAT / Sales Tax registration, filing, and legal documentation must be confirmed by your own tax or legal advisor.

**Changes to the agreed scope are handled through a formal change process.** We will always assess the cost and timeline impact and obtain your written confirmation before any change is implemented.
